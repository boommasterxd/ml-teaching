# Hostinger mail retention

Hostinger documents separate recovery periods for
[messages in the trash](https://www.hostinger.com/support/1583473-how-to-recover-deleted-emails-in-hostinger-email/)
and [a deleted mailbox](https://www.hostinger.com/support/1583466-how-to-restore-a-deleted-hostinger-email-account/).
The [privacy policy](https://mlteaching.boomapps.de/datenschutz) describes the
confirmed periods. Messages can remain in the trash for up to 30 days. Hostinger
can restore a deleted mailbox's messages for up to 30 days before permanently
deleting its data.

**PRIVACY-02 (individual-message backups), accepted.** The maintainer omits
the retention period or deletion criterion for backup copies of individual
messages deleted from an active mailbox. The maintainer has no confirmed
information for this scenario. Hostinger's
[backup documentation](https://www.hostinger.com/support/hostinger-mail-data-location-backups-and-subprocessors/)
describes backup locations. The confirmed periods above remain disclosed.

**PRIV-03 (delivery log retention), accepted open.** Hostinger's
[delivery log documentation](https://www.hostinger.com/support/6404796-how-to-check-delivery-logs-for-hostinger-email/)
describes 30 days of operator access in hPanel. Section 11 of the
[Data Processing Addendum](https://www.hostinger.com/legal/dpa) covers deletion
of customer data after service termination, subject to the applicable service
terms. Retention or a deletion criterion for delivery records during active
service remains unconfirmed. The maintainer accepts this evidence gap and defers
a separate retention disclosure for this processing until a provider source is
available. The public policy describes the confirmed operator access window.
