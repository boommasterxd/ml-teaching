# Children's consent for image training

The operator runs ML Teaching as a private, free educational project. The operator
confirms that the project generates no revenue or other economic benefit for them
and that they do not reuse learner data. The site does not use advertising or paid
accounts. Its educational content is addressed in part to children.

On those facts, the project treats the image-training offer as outside the scope
of Article 8 GDPR. That article applies to consent-based processing when an
information society service is offered directly to a child. The definition in
Article 4(25) GDPR refers to services normally provided for remuneration. Under
these conditions, Article 8 does not require age verification or parental
authorization for this offer.

The model download requires explicit consent and sends connection data to Google
and Kaggle. Camera access requires separate consent. Training images and their
derived features stay in the browser. The privacy policy describes these flows
and their respective legal bases. Reassess Article 8 if the financing, commercial
purpose, audience or data processing changes.
